## Writing as a form of inquiry

This section gathers work led by the practice of writing: essays, criticism, reflections, and pieces that may resist a more precise category. Some will remain close to language and research; others will begin elsewhere.

I approach writing as more than a record of conclusions. It is a way to discover what I think, test the shape of an idea, and attend carefully to experience. The pieces collected here reflect that process as well as its results.
