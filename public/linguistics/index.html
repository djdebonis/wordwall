Linguistics

Essays, studies, and field notes about language by David DeBonis.

Language in form and in use#

This section gathers work in linguistics broadly conceived: syntax and semantics, rhetoric and discourse, public language, linguistic landscapes, language technology, and the cultural conditions in which meaning is made.

Some pieces are formal research projects; others use close reading or the essay as a method of analysis. Together, they reflect my interest in language both as a structured system and as a social and creative practice.
