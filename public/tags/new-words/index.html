Singaid

Singaid (idiom, idiomatic expression) Pronunciation /siŋ-eɪd/ or /sɪŋ-eɪd/ sing as in “sing” or “sin” with a g (depending on dialect) aid as in “aid”—offering help. Defintion Said to someone who has just hiccuped, as a polite remark; a remark offering good wishes of hiccups passing. Like the English expression bless you, which is often used when someone is sneezing, singaid is a polite expression towards someone with the hiccups.
